## Definition

* Someone is a **sentient being**, if and only if it has the ability to be aware or experience goodness, badness (evil), in accordance, in contrast, desirable, undesirable, favorable, unfavorable, and the likes. 
* Someone has a **will**, if and only if it has the faculty (capacity) by which someone decides on and initiates action.
* Something is **necessary (Must, should, etc)**, if and only if it cannot be otherwise if the otherwise will imply wrongness or contradiction.
* Someone is **righteous**, if and only if, it has justifiable or right behavior or characteristic.
* Something is **right**, if and only if, it is in accordance with what is correct (fact or reality) for a particular situation or thing. *Example: this is the right way to the cottage, eating healthy food is right to become become healthy, etc.* 
* Something is **Good**, if and only if it is in accordance with a being. 

**Note:** A definition may vary and not universally applicable depends on the context. But in the context of this meditation or what I mean, those definitions are strictly defined as that.
