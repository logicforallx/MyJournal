# Uncontradicted Goodness

## Why choosing or achieving uncontradicted goodness is more good than the otherwise (not choosing it)? Or why it could be our guide for our goodness optimization, which means to make or realize something good as good as possible?

2. If that is the case, choosing or achieving that goodness adds goodness. *This is base on its implication.*

3. If that is the case, choosing or achieving that goodness is more good than the otherwise (not choosing or achieving it). *This is base on its implication.*

4. Therefore, If I'm choosing or achieving uncontradicted goodness, then it is more good than the otherwise (not choosing or achieving it). *This is base from 1-3.* (Goodness + Another Goodness = More Goodness).
