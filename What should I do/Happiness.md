# Happiness

## Is Happiness a must?

## Definition

* Something is **necessary (Must, should, etc.)**, if and only if it cannot be otherwise if the otherwise will imply wrongness or contradiction. 
* X is **experiencing** something, if and only if something is happening to X and X is aware of it.
* X is **happy**, if and only if X is in good state (situation) and X is experiencing (aware of) them positively. In other words, being happy means having a good, positive, favorable, desirable or in accordance feeling (or emotion) or experiences.

## Logical Reasoning

1. Having a real goodness and experiencing (aware of) them positively is better than the otherwise: *This is because, it implies that it is good objectively and it is good subjectively, which is more good than the otherwise, which implies better than the otherwise.* [Implication]
2. Happiness is a good, positive, favorable, desirable or in accordance feeling (or emotion) or experiences. [Implication]
3. Therefore, happiness is inherently good and better than the otherwise (not happy). [1,2]
4. Therefore, If I must engage in a right and truly good behavior, then should I strive for happiness? [3]
