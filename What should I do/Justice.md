   3. So truly good (rightly desirable or in accordance that is authentic) is logically right to everyone who has desire. [1,2]
7. Evil (undesirable, unfavorable, etc) is logically wrong to everyone who has desire unless it is necessary for the greater good:

   3. Evil (undesirable, unfavorable, etc) is logically wrong to everyone who has desire unless it is necessary for the greater good. [1,2]
8. Truly good behavior to everyone who has desire is logically right behavior or treatment. [6]
