# Formulation of Hypothesis

Hypothesis is like an informed and educated guess. It might be formulated after careful observation, logical reasoning, or an understanding of existing theories. It's an attempt to explain a phenomenon or make predictions about what might happen under specific conditions, or something else.

They offer a clear direction for investigation, preventing aimless exploration and guiding focused research efforts.

Regardless of whether a hypothesis is confirmed or refuted through testing, it contributes to refining our understanding of a phenomenon. 

Even a negative result helps eliminate potential explanations, guiding researchers toward a more accurate understanding.

Therefore, formulation of hypothesis can help us in knowing the truth or right.
