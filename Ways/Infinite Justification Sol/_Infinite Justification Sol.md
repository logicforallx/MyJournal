# Infinite Regress Justification Solution for Proven Absolute Knowledge

## Problem Identification

1. Fact on this meditation means a thing that is known or proved to be true; proven truth, proven reality/actuality, and not opinion. [Implication]
2. Proven means demonstrate the truth or existence of (something) by evidence or argument. [Implication]
3. Facts or proven truth/rightness means demonstrated truth or existence of (something) by evidence or argument. [1,2]
4. Demonstrated the truth or existence of (something) by evidence or argument is also called justification or providing reason. [Implication]
5. Facts or proven truth/rightness requires justification or providing reason. [3,4]
* **If proven requires justification, then how can you address Infinite regress of justification? [5]**  This is because, #5 means that a justification will cause further justification to know if that justification is also proven, which may cause infinite regress of justification. Symbolically, it is like A because B, B because C, C because D, D because E, F because G, and so on, ad infinitum. How this infinite chain of reasoning can lead to absolute certainty of knowledge, if proven absolute knowledge must be proven, and proven requires a justification?
