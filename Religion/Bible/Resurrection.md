# Bible God existence evidence from the resurrection

God existence evidence from the resurrection: This argument states that the historical fact of Jesus' resurrection points to the existence of God. The Bible supports this argument in verses such as 1 Corinthians 15:17, which says, "And if Christ has not been raised, your faith is futile; you are still in your sins."

This argument states that the resurrection of Jesus from the dead provides evidence for the existence of God. The Bible claims that Jesus rose from the dead on the third day after His crucifixion, and that He appeared to many people after His resurrection. The fact that so many people witnessed the resurrected Jesus provides evidence for the reality of His resurrection and the existence of God. This can be seen in 1 Corinthians 15:3-8, which says, "For what I received I passed on to you as of first importance: that Christ died for our sins according to the Scriptures, that he was buried, that he was raised on the third day according to the Scriptures, and that he appeared to Cephas, and then to the Twelve.
