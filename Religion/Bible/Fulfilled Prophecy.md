# Bible God existence evidence from Fulfilled Prophecies:
 
 The Bible contains numerous prophecies that were fulfilled with remarkable accuracy. This demonstrates that the Bible is the Word of God and not simply the work of human authors. The Bible supports this argument in verses such as Isaiah 46:9-10, which says, " Remember the former things of old: for I am God, and there is none else; I am God, and there is none like me, Declaring the end from the beginning, and from ancient times the things that are not yet done, saying, My counsel shall stand, and I will do all my pleasure: ". And John 13:19 which says, "Now I tell you before it come, that, when it is come to pass, ye may believe that I am".

## Here is the logical argument:

The Bible contains numerous prophecies about future events.

Premise: These prophecies have been fulfilled with remarkable accuracy.

    The fulfillment of prophecies in the Bible is considered remarkable because they were made hundreds of years before the events occurred, yet they came true with remarkable accuracy. The prophecies were not vague or general statements that could be interpreted in a variety of ways; they were specific and detailed predictions that came to pass in a way that could not be easily dismissed as coincidence or luck.

    For example, the prophecy in Micah 5:2 predicted the birthplace of a ruler who would come to be known as the King of the Jews. Bethlehem was not a significant or prominent town at the time, and there was no reason to expect that a ruler of any importance would come from there. Yet, centuries later, Jesus was born in Bethlehem and went on to become one of the most influential figures in history.

    Similarly, the prophecy in Isaiah 44:28 named a specific king who would come to power and release the Jewish exiles from captivity in Babylon. The prophecy was made long before Cyrus was born, yet he fulfilled it precisely by issuing the decree allowing the exiles to return to their homeland.

    The accuracy and specificity of these and other fulfilled prophecies in the Bible are what make them remarkable and provide evidence of the divine origin of the Bible.

Premise: While some human beings have made accurate predictions about future events, they are unable to predict all events with certainty.

Conclusion 1: Therefore, the fulfillment of prophecies contained in the Bible is evidence that the predictions are not solely the result of human wisdom or knowledge, but could come from a divine source.

Conclusion 2: The fulfillment of prophecies is a piece of evidence supporting the existence of God, who is believed to be able to accurately predict and bring about future events.

It is important to note that while some humans have made accurate predictions about future events, their predictions are limited and not always correct. 

Additionally, the accuracy and interpretation of other specific prophecies in the Bible may be a matter of debate. Therefore, fulfilled prophecies should be considered alongside other arguments for the existence of God.
