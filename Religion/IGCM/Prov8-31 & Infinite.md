
# Prov8-31 & Infinite

**# IGCM says to look at the doctrine, so let's examine their teaching about Proverbs 8:31:**

IGCM says, *“Don't look at the person; look at the doctrine.”* If we examine the doctrine, though, isn't there something questionable about it? SEB said that God was supposedly in His dwelling place on earth when heaven and earth did not yet exist, according to Proverbs 8:31, and that *“God is not everywhere.”* By logical necessity, is it possible for God to have a dwelling place on earth and for God not to be everywhere? Let me present this logical argument about the nature of the Supreme Being:

---

**Stipulative Definition:**

* **God:** A Supremely Perfect Being.
* **Logical Necessity:** If something is logically necessary, it means that it cannot be otherwise according to the principles of logic.
* **Infinite Existence:** If a being has infinite existence, it exists without any limits or boundaries, whether in time, space, or other logically possible cases.

**Logical Argument:**

1. A being that possesses all perfections is more supremely perfect than a being that does not. *Based on the implication that the whole is greater than its parts.*

2. Thus, a Supremely Perfect Being has all perfections. *Based on #1.*

3. If a perfect being has infinite existence, then it has more perfection than the otherwise: *For If a perfect being has infinite existence, then it exists without any limits or boundaries, whether in time, space, or other logically possible cases. If that is the case,  its perfection exist without any limits or boundaries, whether in time, space, or other logically possible cases. That is more perfection than the otherwise of it.*

4. Therefore, by logical necessity, a Supremely Perfect Being (God) has infinite existence. *Based on #2 and #3.*

If we assume that the Supreme Being is in His dwelling place on earth and that God is not everywhere, wouldn't His existence therefore be limited? If so, wouldn't that contradict the infinite existence of the Supreme Being?

Moreover, if we look at Proverbs 8:31 using the Greek interlinear version, does it really say that He was there in His dwelling place on earth? Isn't what is actually written:

**Rejoicing** *H7832* **in the habitable part** *H8398* **of His earth** *H776*?
