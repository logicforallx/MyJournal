# No truth alone 

## Does truth, true, or right requires something? or theres no truth alone?

## Logical Deduction

1. True means in accordance with fact or reality. [Implication]
2. Right means true or correct as a fact. [Implication]
3. Right means in accordance also with fact or reality. [1,2]
4. In accordance with fact or reality is something, or an existing, or example of existing, at least as an idea. [Implication]
6. Right requires existing at least as an idea, therefore requires something. [3,4]
7. True requires existing at least as an idea, therefore requires something. [1,4]
* **Therefore, right and true requires something, or a reference points.** There's no right or true alone, and it's always dependent on something or it is always dependent on the context. Like Rightly or truly desirable, morally right, truly existing, etc. For instance, if someone said I am the truth, then the question will be "true what?", is it truly existing? truly good or what? It will always depend on the context. 

## Scrutiny

* There can be right answers to hypothetical situations that don't necessarily involve existing things. Truth can apply to abstract concepts or ideas that don't necessarily require physical existence.
    - Those are still existing at least as an idea, and In accordance with fact or reality is an example of existing. So, It must exist at least in the  mind or as an idea. So still, it requires something

## Conclusion

Right and true requires something. There's no truth alone.
