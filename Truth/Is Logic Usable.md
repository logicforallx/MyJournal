    - Quantum mechanics doesn’t break logic — it breaks our intuitions. When physicists describe “wave and particle,” they don’t mean literally “electron = wave AND electron ≠ wave in same sense at same time.” They mean electrons have dual properties depending on context. That still fits logic, just under more precise definitions.
