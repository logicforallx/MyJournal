# Why?

## *Why should I choose and seek truth or right?*

## Logical Reasoning 

1. It is possible that I have an inherent will, as I can observe. [Observation]
2. Will is the same as the faculty (or inherent mental power or capacity) by which someone decides on and initiates action. [Implication]
3. Even if I decide to deny, disbelieve, or doubt observation #1, it is the same as the inherent mental power or capacity to decide or initiate that choice (action). [Implication] 
4. Even if I decide to deny, disbelieve, or doubt observation #1, it is the same as the inherent will that drives that decision. [2,3]
5. It is illogical to decide to deny that I have inherent will or desire that drives my decision or choices, it is in my nature. [1,4]
6. If I have inherent will or desire, then deciding not to choose or act for now is the same as inherent will or choice. [Implication]
7. Deciding not to have a will is impossible in me or in this being for now. [5,6]
8. Those who cannot choose to not have a will for now implies that they must engage in will for now, in other words. [Implication]
9. I must choose, decide, or engage in a will for now, as otherwise is impossible for now. [7,8]
10. True means in accordance with fact or reality; accurate, correct, not false; It is what it is. [Implication]
11. Right means true or correct as a fact; accurate, correct, not false, not wrong; It is what it is. [Implication]
12. I must choose, decide, or do something true or right for now. [9,10,11] 
13. I cannot consciously choose true or right if I don't know (not conscious on) what is true or right.  [Implication]
14. Is it a must for me to know or seek what is true or right to consciously choose, decide, or do it? [12,13]

* For instance, truth can lead to the following:
    1. Better decision-making: By understanding the facts, we can make choices that are more likely to achieve our goals or will.
    2. Progress and innovation: Discovering new truths paves the way for advancements in science, technology, and society.
    3. Personal growth: Seeking truth allows us to challenge our biases, learn new things, and expand our understanding of the world.
    4. Problem Solving: If we don't know the truth or rightness in solving a problem, then we cannot consciously solve a problem. 
    5. Goodness: If we don't know the authentic goodness or truly good for us, then we can't consciously choose what's good for us.
