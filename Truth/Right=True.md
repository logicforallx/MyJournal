# Right=True

## Is right also true?


## Logical Deduction
1. True means in accordance with fact or reality; accurate, correct, not false; It is and not it is not. [Implication]
2. Right means true or correct as a fact; accurate, correct, not wrong; It is and not it is not. [Implication]
3. So in logical sense, right is also true. [1,2] 
* *however, truthness and rightness depend on the context. Like not all true behavior or statement is morally right, but morally right is also morally true. Example:*
    * There are serial killers is a true statement, but not morally right and true. They are different in context, one is a true statement, other one is not true or valid as morally good or justified.
    * Helping each other is often considered as morally right, so often considered true as morally good or justified.

## Conclusion

In logical sense, right is also true. however, truthness and rightness depend on the context.
