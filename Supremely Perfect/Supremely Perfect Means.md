# Supremely Perfect Being Meaning

## What do I mean by Supreme Being or Supremely Perfect Being?

1. Something is **Supreme**, if and only if, it is the highest, greatest or the maximal. [Definition]
2. Something is **Perfect**, if and only if, it is a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). [Definition]
3. Something is **Positive**, if and only if, it is good, affirmative, or constructive quality or attribute. In other words, it is a property that is in accordance with a being. [Definition]
4. Therefore, something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). In other words, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely in accordance (good) with a being and free from faults or defects (badness). [Implication] from [1,2,3]

* **Synonyms:** It is also identical to the following:
    - In other words, it is maximal in perfection.
    - In other words, it is maximal in absolute goodness (positive property) that are free from badness (negative property).
    - In other words, it is maximal in absolute and pure goodness (positive property).
    - In other words, it is the greatest in absolute and flawless good property.
    - In other words, it is the most in total excellent property that are flawless.
    - And the likes.

* **Antonyms:** It is not identical to the following: 
    - Supremely absolute negative being, free from positivity.
    - A being that is maximal in a property that is absolutely negative and full of faults or defects (negative) and free from positivity.
    - A being that is maximal in a property that is absolutely not in accordance with a being and free from positivity (in accordance).
    - Greatest absolute evil being.
    - Maximally most awful being.
    - Maximally most terrible being
    - Maximally good being, but not purely good or not faultless.
    - Excellent being but not the greatest of all.
    - And the likes.
