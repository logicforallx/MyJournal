## Why the partial contemplation here still brings the greatest possible satisfaction in this life?

## Argument from Descartes View 

> Descartes said it is base by experience that even partial contemplation of God brings the greatest satisfaction available in this life

1. In this life, the soul is still united to the body, which limits its capacity.
2. However, through reason, the mind can still conceive of God’s perfection and direct its attention to Him.
3. This act — though partial — is still directed at the highest possible object of thought.
4. Therefore, no other experience or mental act can be more satisfying in kind, even if not in degree.
5. The satisfaction comes from aligning with what is most real, most good, and most intelligible — even briefly.

This reflects Descartes’ rationalism: the intellect is fulfilled not by quantity of pleasure, but by proximity to perfection.
