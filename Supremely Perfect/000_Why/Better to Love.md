# Better to Love

## Definition

1. Something is **Supreme**, if and only if, it is the highest, greatest or the maximal. [Definition]
2. Something is **Perfect**, if and only if, it is a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). [Definition]
3. Something is **Positive**, if and only if, it is good, affirmative, or constructive quality or attribute. In other words, it is a property that is in accordance with a being. [Definition]
4. Therefore, something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). In other words, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely in accordance (good) with a being and free from faults or defects (badness). [Implication] from [1,2,3]
5. If X **love** someone or something, then X deeply like, choose, desire, or admire it, where admire means to look at (something impressive or attractive) with pleasure or with perceiving or feeling its goodness (which implies lovable).

## Deduction

1. If God (Supremely Perfect being) exist, then its nature is the absolutely or the most objectively good: *As we can see in '\Supremely Perfect\' docu*.
2. To love the absolutely or most objectively good implies to love (deeply like, choose, desire, or admire) the absolutely or most objectively good: *Base from its implication.*
3. To love (deeply like, choose, desire, or admire) the absolutely or most objectively good implies to appreciate the absolutely or most objectively good: *Base from its implication.*
4. To appreciate the absolutely or most objectively good implies to recognize and enjoy the good qualities of absolutely or most objectively good: *To appreciate x if and only if it's recognition and enjoyment of the good qualities of someone or something (x), as it define*
5. To recognize and enjoy the good qualities of absolutely or most objectively good implies to experience (to perceive and to happen at experiencer at least by the mind) the good qualities of absolutely or most objectively good: *Base from its implication.*
6. To experience (to perceive and to happen at experiencer at least by the mind) the good qualities of absolutely or most objectively good is better (more in accordance with a being) than the otherwise: *Base from its implication.*
7. Therefore, If God exist, to love (deeply like, choose, desire, or admire) God's nature is better than the otherwise: *Base from 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 above*

## Scrutiny

* But even if God does not exist, if its nature is better to love by logical implication, then it is better, right?
    - You cannot love someone or something that does not exist or is not there, right?
    * But we can still love something like the idea of ideal goodness even if it does not exist actually, does it not?
    - If that's the case, it is still existing as an idea, does it not?
    ---

* Loving an idea is weaker than loving reality or outside idea. Even if you can love the idea of goodness, it is not the same as loving a real supremely perfect being. The effect on the lover is diminished — like admiring a picture of food versus eating the food itself.
    - Granted, the experiential depth differs. Loving a real supremely perfect being would be fuller than loving the idea. However, even if weaker, loving the idea is still better than the alternative (indifference or rejection), because it cultivates orientation toward goodness. Therefore, whether or not God exists, the hierarchy holds:
        - Loving God’s actual nature (if God exists) → maximal better.
        - Loving the idea of God’s nature (if God does not exist) → better.
        - Rejecting both → worse.

## Conclusion

Therefore, If God exist, does to love (deeply like, choose, desire, or admire) God's nature is better than the otherwise?

Further, even if God does not exist or it exist at least as an idea,  does loving the idea of such supreme goodness is still better, because it aligns your being with the highest conceivable good?
