# **Ontological Grounding of Value Argument**  

## **Ontological ground** means:  

> Something is an **ontological ground** if and only if the existence or reality of another thing is *entirely dependent* on it.

**Logical form of "Something is an ontological ground":**

\[
G(x, y) \equiv \text{"}x\text{ is an ontological ground of }y\text{"}
\]
means

\[
\boxed{G(x, y) \iff (\text{y exists only because x exists, and y’s reality depends essentially on x})}
\]

---
