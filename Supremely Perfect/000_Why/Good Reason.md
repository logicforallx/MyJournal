## Does everything happens for a good or greater reason?

**If everything happens for a good or greater good reason is more Supremely perfect than not, then does a Supremely Perfect being cause or allow everything that happens for a good or greater good reason?**

## Stipulative Definition

* Supremely implies the maximum, highest or greatest possible.
* Perfect or Perfection: The condition, state, or quality of being free from all flaws or defects, as logically possible or necessary within a given context.
* Perfectly righteous implies perfectly right or justifiable qualities and behavior.
* Objectively good on this meditation implies rightly or validly desirable that is objective.
* Objective means not influenced by personal feelings or opinions in considering and representing facts. Contrasted with subjective.
* Logical means according to or agreeing with the principles or laws of logic.

## Logical Reasoning

1. If a Supremely perfect being exists, perfectly powerful and perfectly righteous, it wills and acts objectively and perfectly good to everyone who has desire. *Justified in another file.*  [Axiom]
2. If Supremely Perfect being wills and acts objectively and perfectly good to everyone who has desire, then everything that happens (including evil) to everyone who has desire is for the good or greater good reason to them also:
    1. Reason on this meditation means intention or purpose for which something is done or created or for which something exists. [Implication]
    2. Intention or purpose of a being is a will. [Implication]
    3. If Supremely perfect being does not let everything to happen (including evil) with a good or greater good reason to everyone who has desire, then it does not let everything to happen with a good will to everyone who has desire. [1,2]
    4. If Supremely perfect being does not let everything to happen (including evil) with a good will to everyone who has desire, then it is contradiction on a being that wills and acts objectively and perfectly good to everyone who has desire, which is logically impossible. [Implication]
    5. If Supremely Perfect being wills and acts objectively and perfectly good to everyone who has desire, then everything that happens (including evil) to everyone who has desire is for the good or greater good reason to them also. [3,4]
3. If a Supremely perfect being exists, perfectly powerful and perfectly righteous, then is everything that happens (including evil) to everyone who has desire is for the good or greater good reason to them also?  [1,2]
