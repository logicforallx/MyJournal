# Perfectly Powerful 

## Does a Supreme is Perfectly powerful?

**If a being that is perfectly powerful is more Supremely perfect than a being that does not, then does a Supremely Perfect being is perfectly powerful?**

## Definition

* Supremely implies the maximum, highest or greatest possible.
* Perfect or Perfection: The condition, state, or quality of being free from all flaws or defects, as logically possible or necessary within a given context.
* Logical means according to or agreeing with the principles or laws of logic.

## Logical Reasoning

1. A being that has all perfections that are logical or that add up to its whole perfection is more supremely perfect than a being that does not. *As all or whole is greater than its parts*  [Implication]
2. A being that has no imperfect nature (inherent identity) is more supremely perfect than a being that does not. *As a box that has no non-white is more white than a box that does not* [Implication]
3. A Supremely perfect being has all perfections that are logical or that add up to its whole perfection, and it has no imperfect nature (inherent identity). [1,2]
4. Perfection that exists both in one's internal and external is more nearer to supremely perfect than existing in one's internal only, as it is more than. [Implication]
5. Actualization or externalization of ones internal nature is a power or capability. [Implication]
6. If Supremely perfect being does not have a power, then it cannot actualize or externalize their internal nature. In other words, its perfections exist only internally. [5,3]
7. If Supremely perfect being's perfection exist only internally, then it is not supremely perfect. [4] 
8. If Supremely perfect being does not have a power, then it is not supremely perfect. [6,7]
9. If Supremely perfect being has a power, but not perfectly powerful, then still, it has an imperfect inherent identity (or imperfect nature). [Implication]
10. Perfectly powerful is a perfection that is logically necessary to the whole perfection of a supremely perfect being, as otherwise, it is a logical contradiction. [8,9,3]
11. A Supremely perfect being is perfectly powerful, or in other words, it has perfect power nature. [10,3]
12. Having all or infinite power or capabilities that are logical is more perfectly powerful than not. [Implication]
13. Supremely Perfect being is perfectly powerful, so it has all or infinite power that are logical. [11,12]
