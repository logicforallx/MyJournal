 # Is the Supremely Perfect Being Necessarily Exist? Is it Necessary?

## Definition

1. Something is **Supreme**, if and only if, it is the highest, greatest or the maximal. [Definition]
2. Something is **Perfect**, if and only if, it is a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). [Definition]
3. Something is **Positive**, if and only if, it is good, affirmative, or constructive quality or attribute. In other words, it is a property that is in accordance with a being. [Definition]
4. Therefore, something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). In other words, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely in accordance (good) with a being and free from faults or defects (badness). [Implication] from [1,2,3]
5. Something **Necessarily Exist**, if and only if, it exist in all possible world or reality. In other words, its existence is itself necessary, and it could not be otherwise (not exist).[Definition]
6. Something is **Pure**, if and only if, it not mixed or adulterated with any other thing(s). [Definition]
   * **For example:**
   - **Pure water** does not contain not water.
   - **Pure white** does not contain not white.
   - **Pure goodness** does not contain not good.
   - **Pure positive** does not contain not positive.

**Note:** A definition may vary and not universally applicable, depends on the context. But in the context of this meditation, those definitions are strictly defined as that.

## Logical Reasoning 
 
1. Something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in perfection (a property that is absolutely positive and pure - free from faults or defects). [Implication] from [Definition]
2. Maximal in perfection does not lack perfection. In other words, it has all perfection: *For all or not lacking is the maximum of something.* [Implication]
3. Necessarily existing is necessary for more perfection:
    1. If something is necessarily existing and has perfection, then it's perfection exist in every possible world or reality, not just as an idea. [Implication]
    2. If perfection of something exist in every possible world or reality, then it has more perfection: *For P in every W is more P than P in some W or none, by its meaning.* [Implication]
    3. If something is necessarily existing and has perfection, then it has more perfection. [1,2]
    4. Therefore, necessarily existing is necessary for more perfection. [3]
4. So, if a being is maximal in perfection, then it is necessarily existing. [2,3]
5. So, if something is **Supremely perfect being**, then is it necessarily existing? [1,4]
