# Absolutely good

## Is Supremely perfect being an absolute good being and is it absolutely good to all always?

## By Definition

1. Something is **Supreme**, if and only if, it is the highest, greatest or the maximal. [Definition]
2. Something is **Perfect**, if and only if, it is a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). [Definition]
3. Something is **Positive**, if and only if, it is good, affirmative, or constructive quality or attribute. In other words, it is a property that is in accordance with a being. [Definition]
4. Therefore, something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). In other words, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely in accordance (good) with a being and free from faults or defects (badness). [Implication] from [1,2,3]
5. Therefore, **Supremely perfect being** is not only absolutely good but also it is maximal in absolute goodness (positive property) that are free from badness (negative property), and vice versa. [Implication] from [4]
6. Something is **Pure**, if and only if, it not mixed or adulterated with any other thing(s). [Definition]
   * **For example:**
   - **Pure water** does not contain not water.
   - **Pure white** does not contain not white.
   - **Pure goodness** does not contain not good.
   - **Pure positive** does not contain not positive.

**Note:** A definition may vary and not universally applicable, depends on the context. But in the context of this meditation, those definitions are strictly defined as that.

## Is it absolutely good to all always?

1. If something is Supremely Perfect Being, then it is maximal in absolute and pure goodness (positive property), and vice versa. [Implication] from [Definition]
2. If a characteristic is absolutely and purely good (positive) to all always, then it is more absolute and pure goodness (positive property): *For if a characteristic is P (absolutely and purely good) to all always, then that characteristic P actualize to all individual at all instances, which is more than a characteristic P that does not actualize to all individual at all instances, by its meaning.* [Implication]
3. Maximal in absolute and pure goodness (positive property) does not lack absolute and pure goodness, in other words, it has all absolute and pure goodness: *For 'all' is the maximum of something.* [Implication]
4. So, if a being is maximal in absolute and pure goodness, then it is absolutely and purely good to all always. [2,3]
5. So, If supremely perfect being exist, then is it absolutely and purely good to all always? [1,4]
