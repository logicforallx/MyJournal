# Perfectly Righteous 

## Does a Supreme is Perfectly Righteous?

**If a being that has perfect righteous nature is more Supremely perfect than a being that does not, then does a Supremely Perfect being is perfectly righteous?**

## Definition

* Supremely implies the maximum, highest or greatest possible.
* Perfect or Perfection: The condition, state, or quality of being free from all flaws or defects, as logically possible or necessary within a given context.
* Logical means according to or agreeing with the principles or laws of logic.
* Logical necessity implies that if the negation of something is a violation of the laws of logic, then that something is a logical necessity.
* Perfectly righteous implies perfectly right or justifiable qualities and behavior.

## Logical Reasoning

1. A being that has all perfections that are logical or that add up to its whole perfection is more supremely perfect than a being that does not. *As all or whole is greater than its parts*  [Implication]
2. A being that has no imperfect nature (inherent identity) is more supremely perfect than a being that does not. *As a box that has no non-white is more white than a box that does not* [Implication]
3. A Supremely perfect being has all perfections that are logical or that add up to its whole perfection, and it has no imperfect nature (inherent identity). [1,2]
4. Perfection that exists both in one's internal and external is more nearer to supremely perfect than existing in one's internal only, as it is more than. [Implication]
5. Actualization or externalization of ones internal nature is a behavior, as it means the way in which one acts or conducts oneself. [Implication]
6. If Supremely perfect being does not have a behavior, then it cannot actualize or externalize their internal nature. In other words, its perfections exist only internally. [5,3]
7. If Supremely perfect being's perfection exist only internally, then it is not supremely perfect. [4] 
8. If Supremely perfect being does not have a behavior, then it is not supremely perfect. [6,7]
9. If Supremely perfect being has a behavior, but not perfectly righteous (imperfectly or not right or justifiable behavior), then still, it has an imperfect inherent identity (or imperfect nature). [Implication]
10. Perfectly righteous is a perfection that is logically necessary to the whole perfection of a supremely perfect being, as otherwise, it is a logical contradiction. [9,8,3]
11. A Supremely perfect being is perfectly righteous, or in other words, it has perfect righteous nature. [10,3]
