## Does Supremely Perfect Being Possible? 

Does the idea of Supremely Perfect Being even possible? For if not, there's no sense of doing this meditation, does it not?

## Definition

1. Something is **Supreme**, if and only if, it is the highest, greatest or the maximal. [Definition]
2. Something is **Perfect**, if and only if, it is a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). [Definition]
3. Something is **Positive**, if and only if, it is good, affirmative, or constructive quality or attribute. In other words, it is a property that is in accordance with a being. [Definition]
4. Therefore, something is **Supremely perfect being**, if and only if, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely positive and free from faults or defects (negative). In other words, it is maximal in a property that is absolutely in accordance (good) with a being and free from faults or defects (badness). [Implication] from [1,2,3]
5. Something is **absolute**, if and only if, it is not qualified or diminished in any way; total; complete; free from faults or defects. [Definition] 
6. Something is **Possible**, if and only if, it is "not impossible" — it could happen, exist, or be true. [Definition]. However, that definition also depends on the context in which it's used. So these are the different meaning of 'Possible':
    1. Something is **logically possible**, if and only if, it does not violate the laws of logic (i.e., the law of non-contradiction and the law of identity). [Definition]
    2. Something is **physically possible**, if and only if, it does not violate the laws of physics. [Definition]
    3. Something is **practically possible**, if and only if, it  does not violate this actual-world constraints (e.g., resources, abilities, etc). [Definition]
    4. Something is **epistemically possible**, if and only if, it does not violate what we truly know (note that it does not include false knowledge or false awareness like beliefs, assumption, illusion, etc). [Definition]
    5. Something is **metaphysical  possible**, if and only if, it does not violate the fundamental nature of reality. [Definition]
    6. Something is **necessarily possible**, if and only if, it is not impossible or could happen, exist, or be true in every possibility or every possible world. In other words, its possibility is itself necessary, and it could not be otherwise (not possible). [Definition]
7. Something **Necessarily Exist**, if and only if, it exist in all possible world or reality. In other words, its existence is itself necessary, and it could not be otherwise (not exist).[Definition]

**Note:** A definition may vary and not universally applicable, depends on the context. But in the context of this meditation, those definitions are strictly defined as that.

## Logical Reasoning 1

1. If something is necessarily existing, then it must necessarily possible: 
     1. If something is necessary existing, it must exist in every possible world or reality and it cannot be otherwise. [Definition]-[7]
     2. So, if something is not possible and also necessarily existing, then it is logical contradiction, which is logically impossible. [1]
     3. Therefore, If something is necessary existing, then it must necessarily possible. [2]
2. If something is Supremely perfect being, then is it necessarily existing. [Axiom] - base from '\\Supreme Being\Other Nature\Is Necessary.md'
3. Therefore, **Supremely perfect being** is necessarily possible. [1,2]
