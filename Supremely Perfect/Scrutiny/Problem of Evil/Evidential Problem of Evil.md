# Evidential Problem of Evil

## Intro 

The Evidential Problem of Evil is a challenge to the existence of an all-powerful, all-knowing, and wholly good God based on the observation that certain types of evil appear to be gratuitous (i.e., unnecessary or without a greater good justification). Below is a formal argument based on this problem:

## Logical Reasoning

1. If an omnipotent, omniscient, and wholly good God exists, then gratuitous (unnecessary) evils should not exist.
2. Gratuitous evils (instances of evil that appear to have no greater good justification) do exist:
    * **Questionable Natural disaster.** Like tsunami, earthquake, storm surge, etc which killed large number of people including those we perceived as innocent like babies.
    * **Questionable Natural Suffering.** Like inborn disease, slow painful death from cancer and other diseases of people including those we perceived as innocent like babies. 
    * **Injustices.** Like women was raped and burned, then the murderer don't even punish and sent to jail. However, even there's a death penalty or the murderer sent to jail, then what is the justifiable or equivalent good on the side of the victim for the evil they experienced if they are dead already? 
    * **Extreme Poverty and Starvation.** Like poverty and starvation in africa, large number of people especially child are dying or suffering from it. Is it the choice of the child to face that kind of suffering?
    * **Predator-Prey relationship.** This is contradicting desire between each. One just naturally want to continue to live, and they have no choice to address it except to hunt or eat prey because they are natural predator. So, on the side of predator it is good or favorable when they have eaten some prey. However, on the side of a prey, is it favorable or good for them?
    * **Genocide.** Like in world war 2, there are so much death and suffering of a large number of people including those we perceived as innocent like babies. In the holocaust, there are approximately six million Jews were murdered in concentration camps, ghettos, and other locations across Europe. Widespread and systematic human rights abuses, including the use of forced labor, medical experimentation on prisoners, and execution of perceived political enemies.
    * **And many More**
    * ***There is no apparent greater good that could justify these evils.***
3. Therefore, is it unlikely that an omnipotent, omniscient, and wholly good God exists? [1,2]
