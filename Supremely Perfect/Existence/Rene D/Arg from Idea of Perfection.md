# Argument from the Idea of Perfection

## Formal Argument

1. **I am aware that I am an imperfect being** This is because, I'm doubting and doubting is imperfection.
2. **My awareness of imperfection implies that I have an idea of a perfect being** Such as an idea of a being that does not doubt. Base from [1]
3. **A less perfect being (myself) cannot be the source of the idea of a perfect being.** For there must be at least as much reality in the efficient and total cause as in the effect of that cause. This is base from principle of sufficient reason or casual adequacy principle.
4. **The idea of a perfect being must have been caused by a being that possesses perfection.**  For there must be at least as much reality in the efficient and total cause as in the effect of that cause. This is base from principle of sufficient reason or casual adequacy principle.*
5. **Conclusion**: Therefore, does a perfect being (God) must exist as the cause of this idea?

## Scrutiny/Clarification

* Humans often construct ideas about perfect forms or ideals without being perfect themselves.
  - Just because we can construct ideas about perfect forms or ideals does not mean that the total cause is us, right?

## Conclusion

In summary, does a perfect being (God) must exist as the cause of the idea of perfection?
