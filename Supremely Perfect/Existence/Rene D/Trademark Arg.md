## Is Supremely Perfect Being Exist?

René Descartes' trademark argument is a philosophical argument for the existence of God. It's based on the idea that we have an innate idea of God, an idea of a perfect being. Descartes argues that this idea couldn't have originated from our own minds, as we are imperfect beings. Therefore, it must have been implanted in us by a perfect being, God.
