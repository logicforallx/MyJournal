# Evidential Argument for the Resurrection and Supernatural Agency

## **Formulation of the Evidential Argument for the Resurrection and Supernatural Agency**

**Premise 1 (Empirical Evidence of Supernatural Events):**  
There exist well-documented, credible reports of supernatural phenomena, including near-death experiences (NDEs), demonic possessions, and other events that defy naturalistic explanations.

**Premise 2 (Historical Testimony of the Resurrection):**  
Multiple eyewitnesses attested to the resurrection of Jesus Christ, as recorded in early Christian writings (e.g., Gospels, Pauline epistles). These individuals, including the apostles and early followers, endured persecution, torture, and death while maintaining their testimony.

**Premise 3 (Psychological Principle of Martyrdom):**  
It is psychologically improbable for individuals to willingly suffer and die for a claim they *know* to be false. While people may die for sincerely held *false beliefs*, martyrdom strongly indicates the witnesses’ genuine conviction in the truth of their testimony.

**Premise 4 (Inference to Sincere Belief):**  
Given Premises 2 and 3, the most reasonable conclusion is that the apostles and early Christians sincerely believed Jesus was resurrected, based on direct experience or compelling evidence.

**Premise 5 (Best Explanation for the Resurrection):**  
The most plausible explanation for the disciples’ sincere belief in the resurrection—and the absence of naturalistic accounts that adequately explain the empty tomb, post-mortem appearances, and rapid growth of the early church—is that Jesus was *actually resurrected*.

**Premise 6 (Supernature of the Resurrection):**  
A bodily resurrection—a permanent return to life after biological death—is, by definition, a supernatural event. Natural causes (e.g., fraud, hallucination, coma) fail to explain the historical data.

**Premise 7 (General Supernatural Consistency):**  
The resurrection aligns with other documented supernatural phenomena (Premise 1), which collectively suggest the existence of a reality beyond natural laws.

**Conclusion (Existence of a Supernatural Agent):**  
The convergence of (i) the resurrection as a historically attested supernatural event and (ii) corroborating supernatural phenomena provides compelling evidence for the existence of a supernatural agent (God), who alone possesses the power to override natural order.

## **Logical Structure:**  
1. \( P_1 \land P_2 \) (Evidence of supernatural events and resurrection testimony).  
2. \( P_3 \rightarrow \neg(\text{Intentional deceit}) \) (Martyrdom negates deliberate falsehood).  
3. \( P_4 \rightarrow \text{Sincere belief} \).  
4. \( P_5 \rightarrow \text{Resurrection occurred} \).  
5. \( P_6 \rightarrow \text{Supernatural cause} \).  
6. \( P_7 \rightarrow \text{Consistency with supernatural agency} \).  
7. \( \therefore \text{God exists} \).  

This argument synthesizes historical, psychological, and empirical evidence to infer the existence of God as the best explanation for the resurrection and related supernatural phenomena.
