## Definition

* Supremely: The maximum, highest or greatest possible.
* Perfect or Perfection: The condition, state, or quality of being free from all flaws or defects, as logically possible or necessary within a given context.
* Logical: according to or agreeing with the principles or laws of logic.
* Logical Necessity: If something is logically necessary, it means it cannot be otherwise as it means it will violate the laws of logic if it can be otherwise, which is impossible. *Like if triangle is a plane figure with three straight sides and three angles, then triangle without a side is impossible so it must have a side.*
