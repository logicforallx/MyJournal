# Irreducible Complexity


## Argument from Irreducible Complexity

(Wikipedia summary of Michael Behe’s Darwin’s Black Box (1996))

Premise 1: “Irreducible complexity is the argument that certain biological systems with multiple interacting parts would not function if one of the parts were removed…” 

Premise 2: “…so supposedly could not have evolved by successive small modifications from earlier less complex systems through natural selection…” 

Premise 3: “This negative argument is then complemented by the claim that the only alternative explanation is a ‘purposeful arrangement of parts’ inferring design by an intelligent agent.” 

Conclusion: “Therefore the only satisfactory explanation for the origin of irreducibly complex systems is intelligent design.”
