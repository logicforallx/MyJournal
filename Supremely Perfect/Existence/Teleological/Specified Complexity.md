 # Specified Complexity
 
(William A. Dembski, via Wikipedia “Specified complexity”)

**Premise 1:** “It is impossible for specified complexity to exist in patterns displayed by configurations formed by unguided processes.” 

**Premise 2:** “Yet specified complex patterns can be found in living things.” 

**Premise 3:** “One can show by applying no‑free‑lunch theorems the inability of evolutionary algorithms to select or generate configurations of high specified complexity.” 

**Conclusion:** “Therefore living organisms are best explained as the product of an intelligent agent.”
