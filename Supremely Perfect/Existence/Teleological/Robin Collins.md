# Robin Collins’ Fine-Tuning Argument (The Well-Tempered Universe, 2009)


**Premise 1:** Life-permitting conditions require precise values for constants (e.g., dark energy density).

**Premise 2:** The probability of these values occurring by chance is ~1/10^120.

**Premise 3:** The likelihood of a life-permitting universe under theism vs. naturalism is exponentially higher.

**Conclusion:** Fine-tuning is best explained by a cosmic designer.

**Key Term:**
“The Anthropic Principle”: Observers only exist in universes fine-tuned for life.
