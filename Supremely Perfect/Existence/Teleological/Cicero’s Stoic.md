# Cicero’s Stoic Teleological Argument

## (Cicero, De Natura Deorum II. 80 — Rackham translation)

1. “Nothing is of greater magnitude and value than the universe; it follows therefore that the universe is governed by the wisdom and providence of the gods.” 

2. “…all that mighty multitude of objects contained in all the world which are of great service and benefit to the human race” testify to divine care. 

3. “Therefore all things are ruled by divine intelligence and providence.”
