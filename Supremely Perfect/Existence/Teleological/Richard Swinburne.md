# Richard Swinburne’s Inductive Design Argument

## Richard Swinburne’s Inductive Design Argument (The Existence of God, 2004)

**Premise 1:** The universe exhibits order comprehensible to humans (e.g., mathematical laws).

**Premise 2:** Order is a priori vastly less probable than chaos.

**Premise 3:** The existence of God (a being with power and reason) makes order more likely.

**Conclusion:** Therefore, the universe’s order strongly supports God’s existence.

**Key Formula:**
Using Bayesian probability: P(God|Order) > P(Order|Chance).
