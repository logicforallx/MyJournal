# !Something from nothing

* **Something cannot occur without something (from nothing).** Nothing on this meditation is in other words non-existing or absolutely nothing. Something on this meditation means existing thing. Imagine, the apple put or appear on nothing (non-existing), can the apple put or appear on it? Or the apple removes or cease from nothing (non-existing), can the apple remove or cease from it? Impossible, isn't it even in imagination? Since if something happens, occur, or exist with or within non-existing thing then it means "something happen within not happening" which implies "nothing happen, or it does not exist". This is because, something exists with or in non-existing is contradicting in the same context as it implies, and as we can see on the thought experiment below. This is impossible for contradicting on the same context means not logical, hence not truth.
    * **Thought Experiment:**
        * Imagine, you are in the room, however that room is not existing. Contradicting, right?
        * Imagine, you are in the desert with no water (non-existing water), then you say to yourself, I will get water with or in this dessert. Can you get water from it?
        * Peter said that he is in the party right now in chicago, however that party is not happening/existing. So, Is it true that peter is in the party right now?
        * Put your things in nothing, can you put it in non-existing thing? 
        * Fish exist inside the sea, sea exist inside the earth, earth exist inside the solar system, and so on, ultimately exist inside the universe. If ever the universe is not infinite, can it exist within nothing or non-existing?
        * Imagine a scenario where you enter a completely empty place. There is no furniture, no people, no objects — just an empty space. You might describe this place as containing "nothing." But it is still mean that the place itself still exists which means something, right? 
    * **In conclusion,** if we are honest on ourselves, the justification and the thought experiment above shows that Something cannot occur without something or without existing.
    * *Note* that it does not mean Something is not occuring without something, but Something cannot occur without something. It is different.
